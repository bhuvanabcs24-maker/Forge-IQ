## 2. The Status Quo: How Job Shops Operate Today

Precision manufacturing is one of the last major global industries running on legacy, paper-driven habits:

```
[Customer Request] ───> [WhatsApp / Email / CAD Attachment]
                                 │
                                 ▼
                     [Senior Estimator's Desk]
                     • Manually inspects drawing in AutoCAD
                     • Counts holes, cutouts, and bends by hand
                     • Consults 10-year-old printed wall chart
                     • Manually calculates machine pierce times in Excel
                     • Turnaround: 24 to 72 Hours ⏱️
                                 │
                                 ▼
                     [Customer has already bought]
                     [from the competitor who quoted in 2 hours!]
```

### The Critical "Speed-to-Quote" Metric
Industrial procurement studies demonstrate a stark correlation between response velocity and contract win rates:
- **Quotes delivered under 2 hours:** **~60% win rate**
- **Quotes delivered within 24 hours:** **~35% win rate**
- **Quotes delivered after 48 hours:** **<15% win rate**

Job shops lose millions of dollars every year not because their machining prices are too high, but simply because **their quoting pipeline is backlogged by 3 days**.

- **Profile:** Second-generation owner or founder; manages 20–100 machinists, $8M annual revenue.
- **Top Fears:**
  - *Retirement Brain-Drain:* His lead estimator is 61 years old and retiring; nobody else in the company knows how to quote laser cutting and press brake tonnage.
  - *Capacity Idleness:* Million-dollar fiber lasers sitting idle on the second shift because the front office can't quote RFQs fast enough to feed the floor.

- **Top Frustrations:**
  - Spending 75% of the day doing repetitive geometric math (perimeter measurements, sheet nesting, Pierce time multiplications).

## 6. Summary: The Market Opportunity

Precision manufacturing is experiencing a generational changing-of-the-guard:
1. **The Estimator Cliff:** Senior estimators are retiring in droves with no younger generation stepping in to do manual arithmetic.
2. **The Digitization Mandate:** Industrial OEM buyers expect instant self-service digital quoting, identical to modern e-commerce.
3. **The Local Sourcing Wave:** Geopolitical supply chain friendshoring is driving massive volumes of high-mix fabrication back to domestic job shops in North America and allied manufacturing corridors like India.

**ForgeIQ is the purpose-built software operating system that empowers these local factories to survive, modernize, and dominate.**
