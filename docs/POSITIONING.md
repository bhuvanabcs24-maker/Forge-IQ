## 1. The 1-Page Executive Elevator Pitch

### The Problem
Precision contract manufacturing is a **$450 Billion global industry** powered by over **25,000 independent machine shops in the US** and **50,000 in India**. But every single day, shop owners bleed revenue because their quoting process is hopelessly manual:
- Estimators take **24 to 72 hours** to manually calculate laser pierce times, bend deductions, and material costs from drawings.
- **60% of manufacturing jobs are won by whoever quotes first.** By the time a traditional shop returns a PDF quote on day three, the customer has already placed the order with someone else.
- Aggregators like Xometry step into this void, offering instant quotes to buyers while taking **20% to 35% of the shop's profit margin** and stripping away their direct client relationships.

### The Solution: ForgeIQ
ForgeIQ is the **AI Brain for precision job shops**:
- **Instant Quotes:** Customers upload CAD drawings (.dxf, .dwg) or PDFs and receive physics-grounded, production-ready quotes in **2 seconds**.
- **Zero Margin Cuts:** The shop keeps 100% of its customer revenue. ForgeIQ is a predictable, flat B2B SaaS subscription ($500 – $2,000 / month).
- **Physics-Grounded AI:** Unlike generic LLMs that hallucinate numbers, ForgeIQ couples local AI reasoning across **25 manufacturing capability domains** with deterministic mathematical engines (closed-form equations for laser piercing, bend allowances, press brake tonnage, and scrap nesting).
- **Complete Factory Continuity:** Seamlessly tracks the entire order lifecycle from **RFQ intake ➔ automated quotation ➔ machine dispatch ➔ remnant sheet tracking ➔ digital escrow payouts**.
